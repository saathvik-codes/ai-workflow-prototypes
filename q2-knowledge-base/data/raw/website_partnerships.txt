Branch Partnership Benefits

Operational, marketing, and technology support is provided to branch partners.
Partners receive onboarding guidance, co-branded campaign materials, and access to product updates.

Eligibility

Eligible partners must be licensed financial institutions or approved distribution partners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What support do branch partners receive?
A: Partners receive operational support, marketing support, and implementation guidance.

Q: Who can apply?
A: Licensed institutions and approved distribution partners may apply.
