Health Insurance Product Guide

Available Plans

Customers can choose an individual health plan, family floater plan, or senior-support review path. Plan suitability depends on age, city, coverage amount, budget, and medical disclosures.

Family Coverage

Family floater coverage can include spouse and children under one shared coverage limit. The agent should collect each family member's age before suggesting a family floater.

Coverage Amount

Common requested coverage ranges include 5 lakhs, 10 lakhs, and 25 lakhs. Exact premium quotes require advisor review.

Adding Spouse Later

A spouse can usually be reviewed for addition at renewal or through an endorsement request, subject to policy terms and underwriting checks.

Claims Process

For claims, the customer should contact the service team, share policy details, hospital or bill documents, and follow the insurer's cashless or reimbursement process.

Premium Objection

If the customer says the premium is high, explain benefits, suggest checking a lower coverage amount or simpler plan, and offer advisor review. Do not promise discounts.

Existing Insurance

If the customer already has insurance, ask the renewal date and offer a comparison review before recommending any switch.
