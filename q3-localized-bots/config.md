# Language Configuration

## Philippines

- ASR target languages: `en`, `fil`, `tl`
- TTS style: warm female or neutral Filipino voice
- Handling: preserve Taglish and avoid over-translation

## Indonesia

- ASR target languages: `id`
- TTS style: neutral Indonesian voice
- Handling: preserve finance terms such as cicilan, tenor, DP, dan jatuh tempo

## Regional-accent note

The Indonesia prototype is written to tolerate colloquial phrasing and dialect-adjacent wording in customer turns rather than assuming perfect standard Jakarta speech.
