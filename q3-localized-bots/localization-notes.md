# Localization Notes

## Philippines

Use natural business English and Taglish where appropriate.

Examples:

- "May coverage po ba na kasama ang dependents?"
- "Okay lang po, i-check natin yung policy details."
- "For premium reminder lang po, may quick update ako."

Avoid:

- rigid literal translation
- over-formal English that sounds robotic
- switching languages without reason

## Indonesia

Use formal and colloquial Bahasa Indonesia depending on the caller tone.

Examples:

- "Ibu, saya bantu cek jadwal jatuh temponya ya."
- "Kalau ada kendala cicilan, kita bisa lihat opsi lanjutannya."
- "Tenornya masih cocok atau mau dibahas ulang?"

Avoid:

- abrupt English switching
- unnatural Jakarta-only phrasing for all callers
- overpromising on waivers or fee changes
