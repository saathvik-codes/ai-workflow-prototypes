# Conversation Flow

## Recommended call structure

1. Greeting and identity disclosure
2. Reason for call
3. Qualification questions
4. Objection handling
5. Policy or FAQ lookup through KB
6. Decision or handoff
7. Summary and next action

## Example qualification fields

- name
- phone number
- location
- age band
- current coverage or plan
- budget
- urgency

## Handoff rules

Escalate to a human when:

- the caller requests it
- the question is outside the KB
- details conflict
- the caller is upset or confused
- a policy or compliance threshold is triggered
